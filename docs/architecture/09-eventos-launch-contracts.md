# EventOS launch contracts and pilot gates

**Status** — Proposed design. Product, security, finance and legal approvals are recorded in the [implementation plan](../implementation-plans/2026-11-20-companion-backend-architecture.md). Release sequencing follows [PRD v1.1 §20.15](../Unified_Event_Experience_Ecosystem_PRD_Functional_Specification.md#2015-updated-release-order).

## Identity and participant access

Use one principal per OIDC issuer and subject within the launch deployment. A tenant is an isolated customer workspace for private business data. Organisations are commercial actors with explicit memberships; for the pilot provision one tenant per organisation. A buyer can buy as a person or as a member acting on behalf of an organisation. A shared transaction is owned by the selling workspace and has explicit buyer, seller, purpose and allowed actions. The server resolves workspace scope from the target resource and authorizes the actor on that resource. Switching workspace changes the working context, with no new grants. Resolve future cross-region identity and data routing before adding a region.

| Actor | Access |
|---|---|
| Attendee | Own bookings, payment status and credentials, plus public or explicitly invited events |
| Organiser staff | Scoped event administration and necessary registrant fields. Finance, export and event-day check-in are separate grants |
| Organiser reviewer | Applications to their event and submitted document versions only |
| Exhibitor member | Own organisation's bids and applications, their own financial view with the relevant role, and consented leads owned by their workspace |
| Support | Time-limited, case-linked access with a reason, scoped fields and audit |
| Worker | Dedicated service identity with explicit tenant, purpose and scoped permissions. Exports recheck the requester's authority at execution and download |

Use composite tenant-scoped foreign keys for private relations. Store intentional cross-workspace references only in participant records with explicit visibility and revocation rules. Apply allowlisted projections to searches, exports and signed downloads. Recheck permissions on every sensitive request. Test cross-workspace purchase and review, revoked membership during export, and denied direct access to a document owned by the counterparty.

The [attendee and business client design](./10-attendee-business-clients.md) defines how clients select personal or business context.

## Payment and inventory

Persist the checkout, price and terms snapshot, hold deadline, and unique payment attempt before contacting the provider. Keep provider calls outside database transactions. Verify original webhook bytes and durably accept a provider-account-scoped event before acknowledging it. A worker or reconciliation poll normalizes the result and checks the provider account, transaction reference, amount and currency. Duplicate or stale events cannot regress authoritative state or create a second financial posting. Recover provider timeouts using the original attempt and provider lookup before any retry that could move money.

Ticketing and Exhibition own inventory and fulfilment. Commerce owns payment and compensation. A coordinating use case applies verified capture in one PostgreSQL transaction across module interfaces. Lock event sale state, inventory and then booking/payment rows in a consistent order. Recheck sale state, DB clock, booking and allocation ownership after locking. Apply the provider observation, move held inventory to sold, confirm the booking, issue credential records where applicable, and write the outbox in the same commit. Generate delivery artifacts asynchronously. Authorizations remain pending until verified capture, except for an explicitly approved non-payment contract flow.

For the pilot, the hold or winner deadline is exclusive and evaluated at the locked database decision. If a capture is processed after expiry, cancellation or reassignment, keep the booking unfulfilled, record the capture and create one durable refund instruction for the unfulfillable amount. If only authorized, request a void where supported. A failed or uncertain compensation stays visible in a finance exception queue with an owner and reconciliation status. Show the buyer payment received and refund pending until the provider confirms the refund. Product and Finance must accept this cutoff and fund the exception workflow before paid sales. Race tests cover last-unit resale, cancellation versus capture, duplicate capture events with distinct delivery IDs, and late payment by a defaulted winner.

## Booth auction and hybrid

Propose an ascending auction for the pilot. Publish eligibility, increment and reserve, the deadline, deposit/full-payment conditions, and fallback before bidding. Under the same booth lock, authorize direct holds, activate hybrid transitions, accept bids, close the auction, award inventory and process winner expiry. The allocation states that block replacement are Held, AwaitingPayment, Confirmed and Blocked. Use a partial uniqueness constraint and transition expired allocations under lock before creating a replacement. Pending review alone creates no indefinite claim. Use the DB clock after acquiring the lock and require acceptance before the exclusive close timestamp. Persist a monotonic accepted bid sequence and use it as the tie-break if published rules permit equal bids.

Hybrid means distinct booth modes or a disclosed sequential transition. An active direct hold, a winning allocation or an active auction prevents a conflicting transition. If reserve is unmet, close with no sale. At winner default, apply the published fallback once under lock. Eligible options for product approval are return to inventory or offer the next eligible bid at its published price and deadline. Never change the published rules after bidding starts. Where deposits are enabled, define their application, refund and any forfeiture in the approved commercial policy and test the full lifecycle. Route late winner payments through the common compensation path.

## Finance and recovery

Finance must approve the merchant of record, invoice issuer and numbering, tax and credit-note treatment, currency, payout schedule and provider account model per launch vertical. Validate capabilities with the actual account in a provider sandbox or controlled test before paid pilot. Bind each checkout to a versioned commercial-rule snapshot. Foundation includes effective dates, deterministic precedence, preview and an audited approval path. Product and Finance gate subscription collection and entitlements independently from authoring commercial rules.

Record balanced immutable journal lines per currency keyed to a unique business operation. Reserve available refundable balance when approving a refund under a payment lock. Count pending and uncertain provider instructions against that balance. Use entries or explicit reservations to move available seller funds to payout-in-flight atomically when creating an instruction. Use stable instruction IDs, query provider state on ambiguous results, and release only on confirmed failure. Refunds, disputes and reversals after payout create adjusting entries and reduce future payable balance under the approved commercial policy. Track outstanding debits and route exceptions to Finance. Confirm invoices and credit notes from immutable transaction and adjustment snapshots. Reconcile internal instructions, provider transactions, settlement records and bank evidence where available. Daily batch is a scheduling preference for eligible funds. Any pilot deferral needs a signed scope decision and an approved settlement/reporting process.

During restore, pause checkout and refunds/transfers and disable workers that can move money. Restore to the chosen point, preserve the provider feed during recovery, compare all provider activity since that point plus earlier unresolved operations, and reconstruct or reconcile lost local observations before re-enabling writes. Reuse stable provider references and route ambiguous instructions to Finance. Check inventory and booking outcomes, reconcile totals and have Finance and Operations jointly authorize restart. Drill a successful charge and transfer after the restored point and demonstrate zero duplicate effects. If provider coverage is incomplete, keep the affected capability paused.

## Pilot infrastructure

Start with API and worker deployments on managed containers, managed PostgreSQL, object storage and a CDN. Deliver jobs from the PostgreSQL outbox with bounded batch claims, expiring leases, retries and dead-letter handling. Claim rows with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, release DB locks before external calls, and use stable effect keys and reconciliation across lease recovery. Track queue age and retain replay history according to the recovery window. Use PostgreSQL search projections with full-text indexes, trigram matching and scoped filters. Evaluate a separate broker, Redis or a dedicated search service if repeated workload tests miss the approved queue, read or write budgets after tuning, or a required search capability cannot be met. A separate infrastructure decision must record capacity evidence, failure behavior and operating cost.

Measure hot-event checkout contention, same-booth bidding and checkout races, concentrated QR check-in, provider event replay and worker recovery. Product and Operations approve the pilot traffic mix, peak duration, latency and error budgets and reconciliation clearance target before load testing. Start with one organiser and one event that exercises ticket sales, entry, booth purchase, cancellation/refund and settlement. Track time to publish, booking completion, event-day support load and unresolved finance exceptions. Require zero oversells, duplicate financial effects and unresolved material mismatches before expanding the pilot.
